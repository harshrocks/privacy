User Data Deletion Instructions

Application: Instagram MCP • Effective Date: November 2, 2026

In accordance with Meta Platform Rules and Data Protection Regulations (including GDPR and CCPA), this page provides clear instructions on how users can request the deletion of their data associated with Instagram MCP.

How Instagram MCP Handles User Data

Instagram MCP is designed with privacy-by-default architecture:

  • Instagram MCP is a local Model Context Protocol server running locally on your hardware.
  • We do not store any user data, personal details, or media on external or central servers.
  • Your access tokens and credentials remain exclusively in your local environment.

Step 1: Revoke Access via Facebook / Instagram

To disconnect the application and immediately invalidate all tokens granted to the app:

  1. Log in to your Facebook profile associated with your Instagram professional account.
  2. Navigate to Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites.
  3. Locate Instagram MCP (or the associated Meta App name).
  4. Click Remove to revoke all API permissions and invalidate the access tokens.

Alternatively, on the Instagram mobile app or website:

  1. Go to your Profile → Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.
  2. Select the Active tab, locate the application, and tap Remove.

Step 2: Remove Local Credentials

Because credentials are kept locally, remove them from your local machine to complete deletion:

  1. Delete the IG_ACCESS_TOKEN and IG_USER_ID entries from your local .env file or MCP client configuration (e.g. Claude Desktop, Antigravity).
  2. Restart your MCP client.

Step 3: Manual Confirmation & Inquiries

Since no data is retained on external servers by Instagram MCP, revoking the app permissions and removing local configuration files completely eliminates all associated data. If you require formal confirmation or have questions, contact the maintainer:
